High-Velocity Cross-Docking

Under 15-Minute Freight Consolidation & Turnaround

By bypassing conventional long-term storage racks, our cross-docking protocol directly transfers inbound shipments from arrival highway trucks onto outbound corridor trailers.

  • ✓ Zero Dwell Charges: Shipments cleared and re-routed within hours of arrival at Salem terminal
  • ✓ Pallet-Level Scanning: Barcode verification ensuring 100% sortation accuracy without opening cartons
  • ✓ Blast Pre-Cooling Chambers: Rapid core temperature reduction prior to reefer trailer dispatch
  • ✓ Digital Waybill Validation: Instant electronic POD scanning with high-resolution seal photos
