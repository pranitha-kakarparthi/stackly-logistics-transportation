Frequently Asked Pricing Questions

Are port handling and documentation fees included?

Yes, Stackly quotations are all-inclusive. You will never encounter hidden handling fees or gate charges.

How are volumetric weights calculated for air freight?

Air freight uses the standard IATA volumetric ratio (Length x Width x Height in cm divided by 6,000).
