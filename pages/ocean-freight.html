Reefer Container Technology

Active Temperature Logging & Continuous Genset Power

For perishable commodities, pharmaceuticals, and chemicals, Stackly deploys Carrier Transicold & Daikin refrigerated containers equipped with clip-on diesel generators (gensets) guaranteeing uninterrupted cooling during road drayage and terminal staging.

  • ✓ Precision Temperature Bounds: Programmable from -30°C deep frozen to +30°C ambient control
  • ✓ Satellite IoT Beacon: Continuous wireless feed of container internal temperature and supply air
