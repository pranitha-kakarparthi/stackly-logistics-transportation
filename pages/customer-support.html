How often does GPS telemetry update?

Vehicle units and air cargo telemetry ping our central server every 60 seconds while moving on highways or in flight corridors.

Where can I access certified Bills of Lading?

Go to the Order Details tab, locate your consignment row, and click the "Bill of Lading" or "Air Waybill" button to export certified copies.
