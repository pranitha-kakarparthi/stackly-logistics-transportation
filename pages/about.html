About Stackly Logistics

Two decades of industrial supply chain leadership, linking Salem's industrial core to international trade channels.

Our Heritage

Pioneering Intermodal Freight Networks Since 2001

Stackly was founded to eliminate supply chain friction between regional production hubs and global destinations. Headquartered at MMR Complex, Salem, our operations command a modern fleet of over 140 transport vehicles, dedicated cargo aircraft slots, and ocean shipping alliances.

By combining cutting-edge telematics with experienced freight forwarders, we deliver an unrivaled 99.1% on-time performance guarantee across time-sensitive industrial, automotive, and healthcare sectors.

Salem Operations Terminal

The Heart of Our Southern Logistics Gateway

Our primary hub at MMR Complex, Chinna Thirupathi in Salem is engineered as a modern intermodal cross-dock facility. Equipped with 32 automated loading docks, bonded warehousing, and sub-zero storage facilities, the hub links arterial corridors to Ennore, Chennai Port, and Cochin Port.

  • • 250,000 sq. ft. of dry and climate-controlled staging area.
  • • 24/7 biometric dispatch security with RFID seal scanning.
  • • Direct fiber connection to international customs clearance desks.
