Tracking FAQs

Frequently Asked Tracking Questions

How frequently does satellite telemetry update?

Highway haulage updates every 60 seconds; air cargo updates at tarmac waypoints; ocean vessels update via Inmarsat satellite pings every 20 minutes.

Can I share live tracking links with my end customer?

Yes, from the Customer Dashboard you can generate a secure, tokenized tracking link that displays the live stepper and ETA.
