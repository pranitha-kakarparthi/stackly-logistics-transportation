Technical FAQs

Frequently Asked Telemetry Questions

Common questions regarding sensor pings, data retention, and custom ERP integration.

How frequently does satellite telemetry update across transit modes?

Interstate road freight updates every 60 seconds over cellular LTE/GPS. Air cargo updates at departure, cruising waypoints, and tarmac arrival. Maritime containers update every 20 minutes via dual Inmarsat-C satellite beams when out at sea.

Can I share live tracking links with my end customers?

Yes. From your Customer Dashboard you can generate an encrypted, tokenized tracking URL with customized client branding, live milestone stepper, and signed e-POD photos.

How does cold chain temperature monitoring maintain compliance?

Every cold-chain container features calibrated NIST-traceable temperature probes logging readings every 5 minutes. If temperatures deviate by ±0.5°C from pre-set thresholds, immediate SMS and email alerts are sent to our Salem operations desk and reefer technicians.

Is historical telemetry data archived for customs audits?

Yes, complete GPS tracks, sensor readings, and customs electronic clearance stamps are encrypted and archived for 7 years to meet ISO 9001 and ICEGATE regulatory standards.
